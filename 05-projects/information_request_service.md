# Information Request Service

## Project Overview

Information Request Service is a configurable, multi-client document collection microservice designed to automate the process of requesting, collecting, tracking, and managing additional documents required during underwriting and other business workflows.

The service enables organizations to register as clients and configure their own document collection workflows, making the solution reusable across multiple business clients instead of being limited to a single implementation.

---

# Business Problem

Before this service was introduced, underwriters and business teams requested missing documents manually through communication channels such as:

- Email
- WhatsApp
- Phone calls
- Other informal communication methods

This approach resulted in:

- No centralized tracking
- Manual follow-ups
- Poor visibility into request status
- Inconsistent communication
- Increased turnaround time
- Difficult auditability

---

# Solution

The Information Request Service automated the complete document collection lifecycle by providing a configurable platform where document requests could be created, tracked, and managed through a structured workflow.

The service supports multiple clients by allowing each client to configure document collection requirements according to their business needs.

---

# Business Impact

The service transformed a completely manual process into a centralized and trackable workflow.

Major improvements included:

- Automated document request management
- Centralized tracking of document requests
- Configurable workflows for multiple clients
- Reduced manual follow-ups
- Improved visibility for underwriters
- Better auditability
- Faster turnaround for document collection
